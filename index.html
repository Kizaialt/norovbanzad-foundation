Branch One — The Voice

Namjilyn Norovbanzad

Born to a family of herders in Dundgovi province, she became the singer a nation would later vote its Singer of the Century — the definitive voice of Mongolian urtiin duu, the long song.

  1. 1931

    Born 10 December in Ulaan Ovoo, Dundgovi province, to herders Damdin and Namjil.

  2. 1952

    Began her working life as a typist at the provincial court — before a singing voice changed everything.

  3. 1957

    Won gold at the World Festival of Youth and Students in Moscow, carrying the long song onto a world stage.

  4. 1969

    Named People's Artist (Ardyn Jujigchin) of Mongolia.

  5. 1993

    Awarded the Fukuoka Asian Culture Prize for Arts and Culture.

  6. 2000

    Voted Singer of the Century by the people of Mongolia.

  7. 2002

    Passed away, leaving behind a voice that had carried the steppe into the world.
