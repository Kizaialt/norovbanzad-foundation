The Norovbanzad Foundation

The Song
and the Story.

Two lives, one household, two of Mongolia's great oral and written arts — the long song of Namjilyn Norovbanzad and the literature of Namsrain Banzragch, carried forward as one inheritance.

Where the strand divides

She sang the steppe. He wrote it. They married in 1949.

For over half a century their two crafts grew alongside each other — a voice that carried urtiin duu to the world, and a pen that set Mongolian life into prose. The Foundation exists because neither strand is complete without the other.

Branch One — The Voice

Namjilyn Norovbanzad

Намжилын Норовбанзад · 1931–2002 · Long song (urtiin duu)

Born to herders in Dundgovi, she became the definitive voice of the Mongolian long song — and the singer her nation would vote its Singer of the Century.

  1. 1931

    Born 10 December at Ulaan Ovoo, Dundgovi province, to the herders Damdin and Namjil.

  2. 1940

    Entered school at nine. She sang so constantly that classmates named her Duuchin shar — "the singer".

  3. 1942

    Her father died; she left school and returned to herding.

  4. 1946

    Professional debut at fifteen, singing Ider Jinchin at the Revolution anniversary celebration in the capital.

  5. 1949

    Met and married the writer Namsrain Banzragch; the couple settled in Mandalgovi.

  6. 1957

    Won gold at the VI World Festival of Youth and Students in Moscow, carrying the long song onto a world stage.

  7. 1961

    Named Honoured Artist (Gavyaat jujigchin); completed her formal training as a professional singer.

  8. 1969

    Named People's Artist of Mongolia (Ardyn jujigchin). Principal vocalist of the State Song and Dance Ensemble until 1990.

  9. 1993

    Awarded the Fukuoka Asian Culture Prize for Arts and Culture.

  10. 2000

    Voted Singer of the Century by the people of Mongolia.

  11. 2002

    Died 21 December, leaving recordings of Uyakhan zambuutivyn naran, Seruun saikhan khangai and Zeergentiin shil as the reference performances of the form.

Branch Two — The Word

Namsrain Banzragch

Намсрайн Банзрагч · 1925–2003 · Prose, drama

A prose writer from Zavkhan and a laureate of the D. Natsagdorj Prize, he spent six decades setting Mongolian life — the road, the steppe, the ordinary household — into literature.

  1. 1925

    Born in Aldarkhan sum, Zavkhan province.

  2. 1940

    Began publishing short fiction — among the earliest, Jiriin khuukhnuud and Tal nutgiin khavar.

  3. 1949

    Married the singer Namjilyn Norovbanzad; the couple settled in Mandalgovi.
