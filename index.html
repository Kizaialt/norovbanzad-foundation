Branch One — The Voice

Namjilyn Norovbanzad

Born to herders on the Dundgovi steppe, she became the singer a nation would one day vote its Singer of the Century — the defining voice of Mongolian urtiin duu, the long song.

  1. 1931

    Born 10 December in Ulaan Ovoo, Dundgovi province, the daughter of herders Damdin and Namjil.

  2. 1952

    Took a job as a typist at the provincial court — the ordinary life a voice was about to interrupt.

  3. 1957

    Won gold at the World Festival of Youth and Students in Moscow, carrying the long song onto a world stage for the first time.

  4. 1969

    Named People's Artist (Ardyn Jujigchin) of Mongolia.

  5. 1993

    Awarded the Fukuoka Asian Culture Prize for Arts and Culture.

  6. 2000

    Voted Singer of the Century by the people of Mongolia.

  7. 2002

    Passed away, leaving a voice that had carried the steppe far beyond it.
